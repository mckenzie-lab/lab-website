Our specimens enable...

01

Research

Compare morphology, phenology, and location, extract genetic data, verify identifications from previous research, and revisit historical populations to enable research questions that look into the past.

02

Teaching

Study plant diversity, identification, taxonomy, and species distributions directly from specimens. Collections inspire plant enthusiasts of the future!

03

Documentation

Physically preserve observations so they can be examined again as questions and technologies change, and as natural populations are impacted by an increasingly human-modified world.

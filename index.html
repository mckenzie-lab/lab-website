Research areas

01

Speciation genomics and systematics

We study evolution using genome sequencing and computation, as well as fieldwork, collections-based research, and greenhouse experiments. We get excited about any North American angiosperm system, with previous work focusing on Monarda (Lamiaceae), Phlox (Polemoniaceae), and Pedicularis (Orobanchaceae).

Monarda eplingianaMexico
02

Methods and theory

We develop open software and theory for phylogenomics, population genomics, and high-throughput data processing. Our previous work has focused on phylogenomic simulations, theory about the coalescent with recombination, phylogenetic network inference, and selfing rate inference.

03

Natural history at scale

We use fieldwork, herbarium collections, and community science data to document patterns in the world around us, focused especially on flower color, phenology, taxonomy, an biogeogrpahy. We combine this work with technological advances like computer vision and AI to study natural history at unprecedented scales.
